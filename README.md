AUDIO
The site does not include copyrighted Ed Sheeran recordings. Add audio files you are legally allowed to use:
assets/perfect-instrumental.mp3 = background instrumental
assets/thinking-out-loud.mp3 = the song gift
When the song gift opens, background music stops; when the gift closes, background music resumes.
Modern browsers may still require a user tap before audio can play; the candle click counts as the first interaction and the site attempts autoplay.

HOSTING

PHOTOS
Replace the photo files in assets with your own photos before hosting.
